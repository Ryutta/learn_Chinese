I not only like shopping in stores, but also like online shopping.购物gòuwù – to shop, shopping

这件衬衫打折吗？

Zhè jiàn chènshān dǎzhé ma?

She put on her new dress and looks very fashionable.

Zhè shuāng xiézi de jiàgé tǐng héshì de.

其实我更喜欢用现金。

Qíshí wǒ gèng xǐhuān yòng xiànjīn.

Actually, I prefer to use cash.

妈妈正在整理盒子里的玩具。

Māma zhèngzài zhěnglǐ hézi lǐ de wánjù.

Mom is organizing the toys in the box.

朋友们一起去照相了。

Péngyǒumen yìqǐ qù zhàoxiàng le.

Friends went to take photos together.

我买了不少裤子呢！

Wǒ mǎi le bùshǎo kùzi ne!

I bought quite a few pairs of pants!不少bùshǎo – quite a few

慢慢地选择，不要紧张。

Mànmānde xuǎnzé, bú yào jǐnzhāng.

Choose slowly, don't be nervous.

他几乎每天都写日记。

Tā jīhū měitiān dōu xiě rìjì.

Almost every day he writes in his diary.

面对压力要冷静。

Miànduì yālì yào lěngjìng.

Face pressure calmly.面对miànduì – to face, to confront

她后悔没有抱一抱小狗。

Tā hòuhuǐ méiyǒu bào yī bào xiǎo gǒu.

She regrets not hugging the puppy.

闻一闻这朵花多香呀！

Wén yī wén zhè duǒ huā duō xiāng ya!

Smell this flower, how fragrant it is!多duō – how (used to express intensity), many

经过饭店，我想进去吃饭。

Jīngguò fàndiàn, wǒ xiǎng jìnqù chīfàn.

Passing by the restaurant, I want to go in and eat.

失败是成功的一部分。

Shībài shì chénggōng de yī bùfèn.

Failure is part of success.

想一想过去，但要看向将来。

Xiǎng yī xiǎng guòqù, dàn yào kàn xiàng jiānglái.

Think about the past, but look to the future.看向kàn xiàng - to look towards;将来jiānglái – future

他一下子就承认了错误。

Tā yīxiàzi jiù chéngrèn le cuòwù.

He admitted his mistake right away.一下子yīxiàzi – suddenly, in an instant

听听音乐能让人轻松。

Tīng tīng yīnyuè néng ràng rén qīngsōng.

Listening to music can help people relax.

这是我的秘密笔记本。

Zhè shì wǒ de mìmì bǐjìběn.

This is my secret notebook.笔记本bǐjìběn – notebook

请把房间收拾一下。

Qǐng bǎ fángjiān shōushi yīxià.

Please tidy up the room.

关于这件事，我想说第一点。

Guānyú zhè jiàn shì, wǒ xiǎng shuō dì yī diǎn.

About this matter, I want to make the first point.点diǎn – point

到现在为止，全部工作都完成了。

Dào xiànzài wéizhǐ, quánbù gōngzuò dōu wánchéng le.

Up to now, all the work has been completed.到现在为止dào xiànzài wéizhǐ – up to now, so far;完成wánchéng – to complete, to finish

猫咪的毛软软的，用手摸一摸。

Māomī de máo ruǎnruǎn de, yòng shǒu mō yī mō.

The cat's fur is soft, touch it with your hand.猫咪māomī – cat, kitty;软软ruǎnruǎn – soft, very soft

为 (wèi) vs 为了 (wèile)

为(wèi) translates as "for" or "for the sake of". 
            It identifies the person or object that benefits from your action.

Pattern: Subject + 为 + Person/Cause + Verb...

我为你准备了一份礼物。

Wǒ wèi nǐ zhǔnbèi le yī fèn lǐwù.

I prepared a gift for you.

父母总是为孩子着想。

Fùmǔ zǒngshì wèi háizi zhuóxiǎng.

Parents always look out for their children.父母fùmǔ - parents, father and mother;着想zhuóxiǎng - to think about (someone's interests)

这都是为你好。

Zhè dōu shì wèi nǐ hǎo.

This is all for your own good.

不要为这点小事生气。

Bùyào wèi zhè diǎn xiǎoshì shēngqì.

Don't get angry because of such a small matter.

On the other hand,为了(wèile) means "in order to [achieve a specific result]".
                It explains the purpose or intent behind an action. Place 为了 at the beginning of the sentence:

Pattern: 为了 + Goal, Subject + Action

为了学好中文，他每天练习。

Wèile xué hǎo Zhōngwén, tā měitiān liànxí.

In order to learn Chinese well, he practices every day.

为了健康，我不喝可乐。

Wèile jiànkāng, wǒ bù hē kělè.

In order to be healthy (for the sake of health), I don't drink cola.可乐kělè – cola

为了省钱，我今天走着回家。

Wèile shěng qián, wǒ jīntiān zǒu zhe huí jiā.

In order to save money, I'm walking home today.省shěng – to save, to spare

The measure word 头 (tóu)

The word头(tóu) literally means “head”, 
            but in Mandarin it is also used as a measure word for certain large animals, especially livestock or big mammals:

我家有一头牛。

Wǒ jiā yǒu yī tóu niú.

My family has a cow.牛niú – cow

那头猪很大。

Nà tóu zhū hěn dà.

That pig is big.猪zhū – pig

他们看到一头大象。

Tāmen kàn dào yī tóu dàxiàng.

They see an elephant.大象dàxiàng – elephant

农民有两头驴。

Nóngmín yǒu liǎng tóu lǘ.

The farmer has two donkeys.农民nóngmín – farmer;驴lǘ – donkey

我去了农场，看到八头牛、四头猪和两头驴。

Wǒ qù le nóngchǎng, kàn dào bā tóu niú, sì tóu zhū hé liǎng tóu lǘ.

I went to the farm and saw eight cows, four pigs, and two donkeys.农场nóngchǎng – farm, ranch
