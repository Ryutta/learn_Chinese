Don't worry about memorizing all words immediately, you'll regularly come across them during the course.

Just try to recognize them and familiarize yourself with them for now. You can also easily review the vocabulary again later on.

To ask "how many?", there are two question words in Chinese.

Use

几

(

jǐ

) when you expect a small number (under ~10),

and use

多少

(

duōshao

) for larger or unknown quantities.
