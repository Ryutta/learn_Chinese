Practice Lesson 48 (练习 - liànxí)

Wǒ zài kǎolǜ yào bú yào chūguó liúxué.

I'm considering whether or not to study abroad.

她是一个研究生，正在读研。

Tā shì yí gè yánjiūshēng, zhèngzài dúyán.

She is a graduate student, currently doing her Master's.

他在一家大公司实习。

Tā zài yì jiā dà gōngsī shíxí.

He's interning at a big company.

我看到他们公司在招聘新员工。

Wǒ kàn dào tāmen gōngsī zài zhāopìn xīn yuángōng.

I saw their company is hiring new employees.

我想成为一名科学家。

Wǒ xiǎng chéngwéi yì míng kēxuéjiā.

I want to become a scientist.

这是我小时候的理想。

Zhè shì wǒ xiǎoshíhòu de lǐxiǎng.

This was my childhood dream.小时候xiǎoshíhòu - childhood

明天我有一个重要的面试。

Míngtiān wǒ yǒu yí gè zhòngyào de miànshì.

I have an important interview tomorrow.

我的简历写得很好。

Wǒ de jiǎnlì xiě de hěn hǎo.

My resume is well-written.

他有一个惊人的决定。

Tā yǒu yí gè jīngrén de juédìng.

He made a shocking decision.惊人jīngrén - astonishing, shocking

我对花粉过敏。

Wǒ duì huāfěn guòmǐn.

I'm allergic to pollen.花粉huāfěn - pollen

他的前途很光明。

Tā de qiántú hěn guāngmíng.

He has a bright future.光明guāngmíng - promising, bright (future)

她懂得照顾别人。

Tā dǒngdé zhàogù biérén.

She knows how to take care of others.

她说话很直，从不拐弯。

Tā shuōhuà hěn zhí, cóng bù guǎiwān.

She speaks directly, never beating around the bush.

你要好好准备考试。

Nǐ yào hǎohāo zhǔnbèi kǎoshì.

You should prepare well for the exam.

我大学毕业后想去旅行。

Wǒ dàxué bìyè hòu xiǎng qù lǚxíng.

I want to travel after graduating from college.

我决定读研而不是找工作。

Wǒ juédìng dúyán érbúshì zhǎo gōngzuò.

I decided to pursue graduate studies instead of finding a job.而不是érbúshì - rather than, instead of

她打算出国留学两年。

Tā dǎsuàn chūguó liúxué liǎng nián.

She plans to study abroad for two years.

他正在找工作，有点着急。

Tā zhèngzài zhǎo gōngzuò, yǒudiǎn zhāojí.

He is job hunting and feeling a bit anxious.

要是你早说的话，我就帮你了。

Yàoshì nǐ zǎo shuō dehuà, wǒ jiù bāng nǐ le.

If you had told me earlier, I would have helped you.

我的理想是当科学家。

Wǒ de lǐxiǎng shì dāng kēxuéjiā.

My dream is to be a scientist.当dāng - to act as, to serve as

她对未来充满希望。

Tā duì wèilái chōngmǎn xīwàng.

She is full of hope for the future.充满chōngmǎn - to be full of, to be filled with;希望xīwàng - to hope, hope

我们要做好准备。

Wǒmen yào zuòhǎo zhǔnbèi.

We need to prepare well.

他终于实现了自己的梦想。

Tā zhōngyú shíxiàn le zìjǐ de mèngxiǎng.

He finally realized his dream.

我们公司招聘很多新人员。

Wǒmen gōngsī zhāopìn hěn duō xīn rényuán.

Our company is hiring many new staff members.

祝你成功！

Zhù nǐ chénggōng!

Wish you success!

他们在研究一个新问题。

Tāmen zài yánjiū yí gè xīn wèntí.

They are researching a new problem.

我非常激动，今天是我生日！

Wǒ fēicháng jīdòng, jīntiān shì wǒ shēngrì!

I'm so excited, it's my birthday today!生日shēngrì - birthday

他不但没道歉，反而生气了。

Tā bùdàn méi dàoqiàn, fǎn'ér shēngqì le.

He didn't apologize, he got mad instead.

哎呀，我忘记带手机了！

Āiyā, wǒ wàngjì dài shǒujī le!

Oh no, I forgot my phone!

他决定重新开始。

Tā juédìng chóngxīn kāishǐ.

He decided to start over.重新chóngxīn - again, anew

我喜欢数字游戏。

Wǒ xǐhuān shùzì yóuxì.

I like number games.

熬夜的坏处太多了。

Áoyè de huàichu tài duō le.

There are too many downsides to staying up late.熬夜áoyè - to stay up late

运动可以降低压力。

Yùndòng kěyǐ jiàngdī yālì.

Exercise can reduce stress.降低jiàngdī - to reduce

他非常有自信。

Tā fēicháng yǒu zìxìn.

He is very confident.

我很惊讶你会说中文！

Wǒ hěn jīngyà nǐ huì shuō Zhōngwén!

I'm surprised you can speak Chinese!

他连名字都忘了。

Tā lián míngzì dōu wàng le.

He even forgot the name.

你让我很失望。

Nǐ ràng wǒ hěn shīwàng.

You really disappointed me.

她送我一个小惊喜。

Tā sòng wǒ yí gè xiǎo jīngxǐ.

She gave me a little surprise.

我们去吃辣子鸡丁吧！

Wǒmen qù chī làzǐjīdīng ba!

Let's go eat spicy chicken cubes!

我参加了一个培训班。

Wǒ cānjiā le yí gè péixùn bān.

I joined a training class.培训péixùn - to train, training

我收到一份录取通知书。

Wǒ shōudào yí fèn lùqǔ tōngzhīshū.

I received an admission letter.录取lùqǔ - to admit, to accept (an applicant);通知书tōngzhīshū - notification, notice

我在找合适的工作。

Wǒ zài zhǎo héshì de gōngzuò.

I am looking for a suitable job.

他很擅长数学。

Tā hěn shàncháng shùxué.

He is good at math.擅长shàncháng - to be good at, to be skilled in

这是一个难得的机会。

Zhè shì yí gè nándé de jīhuì.

This is a rare opportunity.难得nándé - rare, seldom

他喜欢挑战自己。

Tā xǐhuān tiǎozhàn zìjǐ.

He likes to challenge himself.挑战tiǎozhàn - challenge, to challenge
