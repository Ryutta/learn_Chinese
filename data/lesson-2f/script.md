

Character Insights Lesson 2 (汉字解析 - hànzì jiěxī)

Tip:

You can also use the ◄ ► arrow keys on your keyboard to go back and forth between the characters:

有

yǒu

have, there is

Composed of a hand (top: 𠂇) reaching for meat 月 (yuè, here representing 肉 - meat) below. Having meat in hand meant possessing something.

有人

yǒurén

there is someone

没有

méiyǒu

don't have, there isn't

没

méi

not have, not

The radical 氵 (shuǐ - water) on the left originally related to "sink/submerge." Something sunk in water is gone — hence the extended meaning of "not having."

没有

méiyǒu

don't have

没关系

méiguānxi

it doesn't matter

谁

shéi / shuí

who

The radical 讠 (yán - speech) on the left relates to asking/speaking. The right side 隹 (zhuī - short-tailed bird) provides the phonetic hint for the "ui" sound.

你是谁？

nǐ shì shéi?

who are you?

谁的

shéi de

whose

几

jǐ

how many, several

Simplified from 幾. The simplified form 几 is a pictograph of a small table or stool. As a question word it asks "how many" (for small numbers, typically under 10).

几个

jǐ gè

how many

几点

jǐ diǎn

what time

多

duō

many, much

Two 夕 (xī - evening/moon) stacked on top of each other. Two moons together suggest "many" — seeing the moon night after night implies abundance.

多少

duōshǎo

how many, how much

很多

hěnduō

very many

少

shǎo

few, little

Looks like 小 (xiǎo - small) with one stroke removed — even less than small means "few." It can also be read shào meaning "young."

多少

duōshǎo

how many, how much

少数

shǎoshù

minority, a few

在

zài

at, in, be at

The radical 土 (tǔ - earth) at the bottom grounds the meaning — being located on the earth. The top part 才 (cái) provides a loose phonetic hint.

在家

zàijiā

at home

在哪儿

zài nǎr

where (at)

哪

nǎ

which, where

The radical 口 (kǒu - mouth) on the left indicates a question word spoken aloud. The right side 那 (nà - that) provides the phonetic hint for the "na" sound.

哪里

nǎlǐ

where

哪个

nǎge

which one

里

lǐ

inside, village

Composed of 田 (tián - field) on top and 土 (tǔ - earth) below. Fields and earth together represent a village or the inside of a settlement.

哪里

nǎlǐ

where

这里

zhèlǐ

here

儿

ér

child, son

A pictograph of a child with an open fontanelle (soft spot on a baby's head). Simplified from 兒. Also used as a suffix in northern Mandarin (儿化 érhuà) to soften words.

儿子

érzi

son

女儿

nǚ'ér

daughter

天

tiān

sky, day

A person 大 (dà - big) with a horizontal line above their head representing what is above — the sky. By extension it also means "day" (one cycle of the sky).

今天

jīntiān

today

明天

míngtiān

tomorrow

今

jīn

now, today

The top part 亼 represents a lid or cover coming down, suggesting "the present moment is upon us." It captures the idea of the here and now pressing down on the current time.

今天

jīntiān

today

今年

jīnnián

this year

明

míng

bright, tomorrow

Composed of 日 (rì - sun) on the left and 月 (yuè - moon) on the right. Sun and moon together create "bright." Combined with 天, it means "the bright day ahead" — tomorrow.

明天

míngtiān

tomorrow

明白

míngbai

to understand

家

jiā

home, family

The radical 宀 (mián - roof) on top represents a house. Below is 豕 (shǐ - pig) — in ancient China, a pig under a roof signified a prosperous household and home.

在家

zàijiā

at home

大家

dàjiā

everyone

妈

mā

mom, mother

The radical 女 (nǚ - woman) on the left indicates a female relation. The right side 马 (mǎ) is purely phonetic, giving the "ma" sound.

妈妈

māma

mom

大妈

dàmā

auntie (older woman)

爸

bà

dad, father

The top part 父 (fù - father) gives the meaning. The bottom part 巴 (bā) provides the phonetic hint for the "ba" sound.

爸爸

bàba

dad

爸妈

bàmā

mom and dad

亲

qīn

parent, close/intimate

The simplified form derives from 親. The top component 立 (lì - stand) and 木 (mù - tree) below suggest standing close together like trees, evoking closeness and kinship.

父亲

fùqīn

father

母亲

mǔqīn

mother

母

mǔ

mother, female

A pictograph depicting a kneeling woman with two dots representing breasts, symbolizing a nursing mother. It is itself a radical used in characters related to motherhood.

母亲

mǔqīn

mother

母语

mǔyǔ

mother tongue

父

fù

father

A pictograph of a hand holding a stick or axe, representing a father's authority and role as provider. It serves as a radical in characters like 爸.

父亲

fùqīn

father

父母

fùmǔ

parents

哥

gē

older brother

Composed of two stacked 可 (kě) components. Originally meant "song" (now written 歌), the meaning shifted to "elder brother" — think of the older brother as the one who leads the singing.

哥哥

gēge

older brother

大哥

dàgē

eldest brother, bro

姐

jiě

older sister

The radical 女 (nǚ - woman) on the left indicates a female person. The right side 且 (qiě) provides a loose phonetic hint and originally meant "ancestor," linking to seniority.

姐姐

jiějie

older sister

小姐

xiǎojiě

miss, young lady
